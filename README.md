# Gold Standard Growth

This is a prodjekt for a client that is a USA based marketing firm

---

Build a modern, professional marketing website for **Web Marketing Group LLC**, a US-based digital marketing agency. The site will live at **webmarketinggroup.io** and must be in English.

## Company overview
Web Marketing Group helps businesses grow their online presence through:
- **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization)
- **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization) — optimizing content so it gets picked up and cited by AI answer engines and chatbots (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, etc.)
- **Competitor analysis** — analyzing competitors to identify content gaps and opportunities, and building a data-driven content strategy around it

Their signature offer is a **free audit**: a no-cost review of a prospective client's website, social media presence, and Google Business Profile (Google My Business), delivered as an actionable report.

## Site structure
1. **Header** — logo, nav (Services, Free Audit, About, Contact), sticky on scroll, prominent "Get Your Free Audit" button
2. **Hero section** — bold headline about growing visibility through SEO & AEO, short supporting subheadline, primary CTA button ("Get Your Free Audit") and secondary CTA ("See Our Services")
3. **Services section** — three feature cards:
   - SEO: technical + content optimization to rank higher on Google
   - AEO: optimizing content so AI assistants and answer engines surface and cite the business
   - Competitor & Content Strategy: competitor gap analysis translated into a content roadmap
4. **Free Audit section** (key conversion section) — explain what's included: Website Audit, Social Media Audit, Google Business Profile Audit. Include a short lead-capture form (Name, Email, Website URL, Business Name) with a clear "Claim My Free Audit" submit button. Frame it as fast, no-obligation, and actionable.
5. **Why Us / Process section** — simple 3–4 step visual of how they work (Audit → Strategy → Implementation → Growth/Reporting)
6. **About section** — short trust-building copy about the agency's approach and results focus
7. **Contact section** — must clearly display:
   - Web Marketing Group LLC
   - 1309 Coffeen Avenue, Suite 3056, Sheridan, WY 82801
   - invoice@webmgllc.com
   - Include a contact form as well as the raw details (address + email) visibly, not hidden behind a click
8. **Footer** — logo, nav links, contact details repeated, social icons, copyright line

## Design direction
- Modern, premium, tech-forward feel — not a template-looking agency site
- **Color palette**: primarily deep black/near-black backgrounds with warm gold/bronze accents (gradient gold works well for headlines, buttons, icons), off-white text for readability
- **Logo concept**: a minimalist, modern reinterpretation of a compass/target mark — thin circular rings with a central star or arrow point, rendered as a clean line-art icon (not the busier ornate version), paired with "WEB MARKETING GROUP" in a clean modern sans-serif with wide letter-spacing. Keep it geometric and simple enough to work as a favicon too.
- **Typography**: modern sans-serif (e.g. something like Inter, Space Grotesk, or similar) for headings with generous letter-spacing on the wordmark; clean readable sans-serif for body text
- Subtle motion/hover animations on buttons and cards, smooth scroll, generous whitespace
- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Include subtle SEO-relevant touches: proper heading hierarchy, meta title/description placeholders, fast-loading structure

## Tone of copy
Confident, results-driven, no fluff. Speaks to business owners who want measurable growth in search visibility and AI-search visibility, not marketing jargon.
